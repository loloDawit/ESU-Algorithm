# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith, and give them the
benefit of the doubt before you give them your opinion.

## What is expected

- Be respectful in disagreement. Technical arguments are welcome; making them
  about the person is not.
- Accept that people have different levels of experience. This project began as
  a university assignment, and questions that sound basic are welcome here.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, and move on without drama.

## What is not

Harassment, insults, and personal attacks. Sexualised language or imagery.
Publishing anyone's private information. Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Reporting

Raise it with the maintainer through a GitHub issue, or privately through the
contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled discreetly.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that do not follow this
document, and may block accounts for repeated or serious behaviour.
